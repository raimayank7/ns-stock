Stock register format, with a worked example

The columns of a stock register, a filled-in example for one item, and how the stock summary for all items is made from it.

What a stock register shows

A stock register (also called a stock book or inventory register) keeps one page per item. Every time goods come in or go out, one line is written with the date, the document and the quantity, and the balance in hand is carried forward. At any moment it tells you how much of each item you should have on the shelf.

ColumnWhat goes in it
DateDate of the bill, return or adjustment.
ParticularsDocument and party, for example "Purchase bill 1452 · Gupta Mills" or "Invoice INV/26-27/031 · Sharma Store".
InQuantity received: purchases, goods returned by customers, opening stock.
OutQuantity gone: sales, goods returned to suppliers, damage, own use, samples, gifts, losses.
BalanceQuantity in hand after this line.

Worked example

A shop starts the year with 10 bags of rice of 25 kg, bought at ₹1,000 a bag.

DateParticularsInOutBalance
01-Apr-2026Opening stock1010
05-Apr-2026Purchase bill 1452 · Gupta Mills2030
10-Apr-2026Invoice INV/26-27/031 · Sharma Store228
12-Apr-2026Credit note CN/26-27/002 (goods returned)210
15-Apr-2026Stock adjustment (damaged)19

The closing balance is 9 bags. If you count the godown and find 8, one bag is missing: record it as "Short on count" so the books match the shelf, and find out why.

A summary for all items

The stock summary puts one line per item: opening, in, out, adjusted, closing, average cost and value. Its total is your closing stock for the profit and loss account and the balance sheet. The tool prints both the summary and each item's register, and downloads the summary for Excel.

Here it fills itself. Purchase bills and returns from the Nivesh Sahayak App, self invoices, invoices, cash memos and credit notes for returned goods are read from your saved data. You only type the opening stock and the few movements that have no bill.
